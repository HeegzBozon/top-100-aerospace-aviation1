Governance

We don't rank.
We measure.

TOP 100 Aerospace & Aviation is a measurement platform and a verified professional index. It is not an awards list, and selection is not for sale. This page states how that separation is enforced.

Structural separation

What commercial activity cannot touch

TOP 100 operates commercial service lines, including Ground Control, memberships, and sponsorships. Those exist alongside selection, never inside it. Four conditions hold that line.

One — Selection is blind to billing at the data layer

Scoring and selection read nomination, evaluation, and voting records. They do not read payment, subscription, sponsorship, or membership records. No billing field is an input to any scoring layer.

Two — No paid placement, ever

No position in the index, and no inclusion in it, can be purchased, accelerated, or influenced by spending. There is no sponsored slot and no paid tier of recognition.

Three — Voting is blind and comparative

Selection runs on community nomination followed by blind pairwise comparison. Voters compare candidates without seeing scores, standing, or commercial relationship.

Four — The record is permanent and auditable

Nominations, evaluations, and votes are retained as a permanent record. Selection outcomes are reconstructable from that record rather than from editorial discretion.

Method

How selection actually works

  1. Community nominationAnyone may nominate, from anywhere, at no cost. Nominations stay open year round.
  2. VerificationIdentity, role, and claimed record are checked before a candidate enters the comparison pool.
  3. Blind pairwise votingCandidates are compared against one another rather than scored in isolation, without visible standing.
  4. Multi-layer measurementPerception, objective achievement, subject-matter expert evaluation, narrative influence, and normalization are combined. No single layer decides an outcome.
  5. Permanent recordThe result is published as a verified profile and retained as a record, not reissued as a yearly ranking.
Non-endorsement statement

Commercial service lines of TOP 100 Aerospace & Aviation, including Ground Control, memberships, sponsorships, and merchandise, are independent of the TOP 100 selection process. Purchasing any product or service does not affect, accelerate, or guarantee nomination, evaluation, inclusion, or standing in any TOP 100 index. The selection process is governed separately and is not for sale.
