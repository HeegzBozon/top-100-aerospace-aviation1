Governance

We do not run a popularity contest. We maintain a verified reputation graph for aerospace and aviation, and we publish what it measures.

What this institution is

TOP 100 in Aerospace & Aviation is a measurement platform. Each cycle produces a Volume: a permanent, published record of measured standing across eight professional domains. A Volume is immutable once finalized. We correct errors of fact by annotation, never by silent revision.

Standards of selection

  • Verification precedes measurement. A record enters the graph only after its claims are checked against primary or institutional sources.
  • Measurement is multi-layer. No single signal — social, editorial, or otherwise — determines standing.
  • Domains are normalized against themselves. A propulsion engineer is measured against propulsion, not against policy.
  • Bands are visible. Weights are not. Influence tier bands are published. The formulas that produce them are withheld, permanently, to protect the graph from optimization.

Correction and dispute

Fellows and Nominees may contest any verified claim on their own record. Contested entries are marked as under review while adjudicated, and the outcome is recorded. Standing is never adjusted as a courtesy, a settlement, or a condition of any commercial arrangement.
