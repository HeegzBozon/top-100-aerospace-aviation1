Governance

How we measure

Individuals rank. The institution measures. These are the layers that produce a Fellow's standing, and the reasons we publish bands rather than formulas.

The layers

Verified record

Career history, credentials, and institutional affiliation, checked against primary sources. This is the foundation of every record and the precondition for measurement.

Objective achievement

Documented output: programs led, systems deployed, patents granted, publications and citations, missions and flight record. Evidence-bearing, not self-asserted.

Domain evaluation

Structured assessment by subject-matter reviewers working inside the relevant domain, with conflicts of interest declared and recused.

Peer and community standing

Attributed endorsements from named members of the community. Endorsements are signed, moderated, and never anonymous, because attribution is what makes them worth anything.

Ecosystem contribution

Mentorship, pipeline building, advocacy, and outreach — the work that expands the field rather than any single career inside it.

Normalization

Each record is measured against its own domain and career stage. Cross-domain comparison without normalization produces nonsense, so we do not do it.

What carries no weight

  • Personal lists. A Fellow's own ordered list of peers is personal expression and carries zero measurement weight.
  • Availability state. Whether a Fellow is open to introductions never affects standing or directory position.
  • Any commercial relationship. Patronage, membership, and engagements are structurally firewalled from selection. See the firewall statement.
  • Audience size as a proxy for authority. Community reach is one observed signal among many, never a substitute for verified record.

Bands visible, weights closed

We publish influence tier bands so that standing is legible. We do not publish the weights that produce them, and we will not. A published formula becomes a target, and a targeted formula measures gaming rather than merit. This is a permanent posture, not a transitional one.

Cadence

Measurement runs continuously as records are verified and evidence arrives. Standing is finalized once per cycle and published as a Volume. A finalized Volume is immutable; subsequent change appears in the next Volume, not retroactively in the last one.
