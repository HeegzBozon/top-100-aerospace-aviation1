Governance

Commercial firewall

Recognition here cannot be bought, and it cannot be bought back. This page states that plainly, and is referenced by our patronage and program agreements.

Inclusion in a Volume, standing within the reputation graph, and placement in the directory are determined solely by verified record and measured evaluation. No payment, patronage, membership, subscription, engagement, or introduction influences any of them.

Non-endorsement

Recognition of an individual is not an endorsement of their employer, and patronage of this institution is not an endorsement of a Patron of Record by any Fellow. A Patron of Record supports the publication of the record. They do not shape it, review it in advance, or receive any consideration within it.

What is structurally separated

  • Selection reads no commercial field. Discovery, directory position, and featured placement never read transaction, payment, availability, or patronage data — not for sorting, not for filtering, not for promotion.
  • Patronage is disclosed, never embedded. Patrons of Record appear as attribution. They never appear as standing.
  • The availability layer carries no take rate. We introduce professionals to one another. We do not broker, price, rate, or intermediate their work, and we take no percentage of it.
  • Staff with commercial responsibility hold no selection authority. The people who maintain patron relationships do not adjudicate records.

Consequences

Any attempt to obtain, trade, or influence standing through commercial means is grounds for removal from the graph and annotation of the record. This applies to Fellows, Nominees, Patrons of Record, and staff without exception.

Immutability

A finalized Volume is a permanent record. It is not amended, delisted, or withdrawn on commercial request. Errors of fact are corrected by annotation, and the correction is itself part of the record.
