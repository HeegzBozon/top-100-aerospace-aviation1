Weighing the season

You have been invited to weigh the season.

Endorsing puts a name forward. Balloting decides which names stand. Fellows are shown two nominees at a time and keep the stronger record, one pairing at a time — no leaderboard, no running tally in front of you, and nothing to game. The institution measures; individuals rank.

  • 1One pairing at a time, judged on the record in front of you.
  • 2Every set you record is kept to your own contribution record.
  • 3The measure is public. The formulas are not.
