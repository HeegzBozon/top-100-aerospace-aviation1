## #42 — The Operating Brief (focus)
*Business context, product-solution vision, architecture vision, development practices.*

1. **Opening — why this issue exists.** Seven weeks to the record. The institution
   explains itself before it publishes.
2. **Business context.**
   - The problem: aerospace reputation is unmeasured, fragmented, and social-first.
   - The position: a verified reputation graph and a measurement platform — not an
     awards list. Individuals rank; the institution measures.
   - Who it serves: across the career lifecycle (student, professional, retiree) and
     business scale (founder, CEO, team, enterprise).
   - The economics: introduction-only availability, zero take rate; Patron of Record
     attribution; featured placement is editorial only.
3. **Product-solution vision.**
   - Three stacked layers: Identity (the Fellow profile as home) → Affiliation (The
     Eight) → Availability (introductions).
   - Surfaces and their canonical records: Directory, Newsstand, Chamber, Conferences,
     Colony campus, Seasonal Planning.
   - Personalization as variants, never freedom.
4. **Architecture vision.**
   - One surface → one canonical entity.
   - The machine: handheld (wheel and radio faces) and desk shells; every surface is an
     application the machine runs.
   - The governance firewall: discovery never reads payment, availability, or
     sponsorship; personal lists carry zero measurement weight.
   - Crawlable front door: prerendered profiles, sitemap, AI-crawler policy.
   - Sealed ballots and immutable archives.
5. **Development practices.**
   - Seasonal planning: Program Increments, the PI board, release notes as the public
     ledger of what shipped.
   - Define → Build → Test → Deploy gates; mobile-first; required loading/empty/error states.
   - Release notes discipline: every sprint ends with a dated note.
6. **Planning context.** Where the season stands: remaining sprints, the voting window,
   the volume lock date, open risks (ballot cohort lookup, data normalization,
   bio completeness).
7. **Breakout instructions.** Four breakouts, each with an owner, a deliverable and a
   due issue:
   - **Ballot & Measurement** → verifies the sealed ballot before #43.
   - **Record Completeness** → closes thin profiles and photos before #44.
   - **Editorial Automation** → article/page pipeline green before #44.
   - **Volume Production** → layout, credits, colophon locked before #45.
   Each breakout reports in Seasonal Planning → Releases.
   **Handoff →** #43.

## #43 — How We Measure (focus)
1. **Opening.** Voting opens. What a ballot is, and what it is not.
2. **The ballot.** Pairwise and ranked-choice; the sealed ballot; one voter, one record.
3. **What is measured, and what never is.** Visible tier bands, hidden weights; the
   firewall restated in plain language.
4. **Integrity.** Deduplication, test-ballot flags, cohort integrity, audit ledger.
5. **The voter's guide.** How to evaluate: evidence, trajectory, ecosystem contribution.
6. **Your Eight vs. the measurement.** Personal lists are expression, never weight.
7. **Calendar.** Voting window open → close dates in PT; reminders cadence.
8. **Breakout report-back.** Ballot & Measurement sign-off.
   **Handoff →** cast your ballot; #44 explains what happens to the record next.

## #44 — The Editorial Machine (focus)
1. **Opening.** Voting closes. From ballot to page.
2. **The pipeline.** Nominee record → editorial article → magazine page → volume.
3. **Automation, governed.** What the system drafts; what an editor approves; reserved
   results placeholders until official measurement lands.
4. **Credits and sources.** Attribution, sources ledger, writer/photographer credit.
5. **Record completeness.** Claim your profile; headshot; bio; proudest achievement —
   the last window to be represented well in the volume.
6. **Production calendar.** Freeze dates: copy (Dec 14), layout (Dec 18), lock (Dec 21).
7. **Breakout report-back.** Record Completeness + Editorial Automation sign-off.
   **Handoff →** #45, the eve.
