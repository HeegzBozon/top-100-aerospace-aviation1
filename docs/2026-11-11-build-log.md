# Build log — 11 November 2026

Three passes today: the schema was closed to the public, the PDF engine was moved to a
supported release, and Issue 42 was written, laid down and published. Two of the three are
commits; the third is live records.

---

## 1. The schema, closed

*00:47 — two passes over `base44/entities/`.*

Row-level security was applied to **178 of the 262 entity schemas**. Until today most tables
were readable by any signed-in account, which for operational tables is the same as open.

Three guard shapes were used, never a fourth:

- **Admin-only** — the operational desks: timecards, invoices, mail issues, scoring and
  season machinery. 247 schemas now carry an admin branch somewhere in their rules.
- **Owner-or-admin** — anything a Fellow writes about themselves or their own work:
  125 scoped by the owner's email (`fellow_email`, `claimer_email`, `reporter_email`,
  `author_email`), 15 by `created_by_id`.
- **Open read, writes held** — 14 schemas, and only where every field is meant for the
  public internet and nothing personal or payment-bearing is stored: published
  `MagazinePage`, published `MagazineIssue`, `ColonyMapLayout`, and their kin. Writes stay
  admin-only on every one of them.

Reads that a visitor submission needs are still open on create, with the record readable
only by its submitter or the masthead — a report, an inquiry, a referral answer.

**Six schemas are deliberately unguarded:** `User` (platform-owned) plus five session and
game tables — `AgendaItem`, `HuddleSession`, `LiveReaction`, `NomineeCard`, `SessionAction`.
They are listed below as work, not as an oversight.

## 3. Issue 42: written, laid down, published

*05:37 — the authoring pass. Live records — the publishing pass.*

Issue 42 is not composed page by page in the Studio. It is **authored as one document in
code**, and the Studio lays it down.

- **`src/components/magazine/briefs/issue42.js`** (430 lines) — the issue's whole self:
  identity (title, subtitle, kicker, preface, colophon, contributors), three sections, six
  articles, and sixteen pages of laid-out blocks.
- **`src/components/magazine/briefs/index.js`** — a registry, so a written issue is found by
  its number and an unwritten one simply has no brief.
- **`src/components/magazine/bindBrief.js`** — lays a brief down: replaces the issue's pages
  and articles, links each page to the article it opens, builds article bodies from the body
  blocks, sets each article's opening page, and writes the identity onto the issue. **The
  issue record itself is kept** — its number, its status, its links.
- **`src/components/magazine/IssueBlueprint.jsx`** — a *Written brief* panel in the Blueprint
  tab, with one key: *Lay down the brief*. It shows the issue it just laid down, not the one
  it replaced.

Then the live issue was bound and published:

| | |
|---|---|
| Issue | `Algorithms, Alchemy, and Aerospace` — an invitation to join the Season 4 Selection Committee |
| Pages | 16, replacing the 17 previously on the issue |
| Articles | 6, in reading order |
| Chapters | The Alchemy, The Work Ahead |
| Calls to action | 4 — the ballot twice, a diary link, the record |

Published in the same pass: the issue, all sixteen pages and all six articles, so
`/magazine/6ae92a22cc8d8da9719614f8` reads without an account. The cover is set in type
rather than as a plate, so the cinematic opening still opens on the invitation.
