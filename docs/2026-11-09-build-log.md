# Build log — 9 November 2026

What moved on the machine today, in three passes: the committee's desk in the morning, the
hardware through the afternoon, and the Colony's seat on the desk at the close. Every item
below is in the codebase, built, and committed.

---

## 1. The Manifest, the standings and the nomination ladder

*Morning — 01:45 to 04:22.*

The morning went to the two surfaces an Analyst actually works: the Manifest (what a Fellow
is choosing) and the standings (how their choosing is reported back to them).

**Manifest and measurement**
- Masthead and layout redesigned, twice — the second pass is the one that stands.
- The measure was rebuilt as a copper instrument rather than a counter, with the surplus
  beyond 100 printed in rose gold. Capacity raised to 250, with 100 kept as the primary
  target. The list title is fixed platform copy, not an editable field.
- A countdown and a status banner were seated under the masthead so a Fellow knows where the
  cycle stands without leaving the surface.
- Category tabs and the media block were brought into line with the new masthead rhythm.

**Standings and the personal record**
- The standings counter was replaced by a personal TOP 100 view — the reading a Fellow
  actually wants, rather than a tally of marks.
- Names now survive selection: a chosen entry keeps its row after it is taken, and the
  confirmation is shown in place instead of the row disappearing.
- The Crosscheck tab was added to personal standings, and the Manifest's key visibility was
  corrected so the tab strip no longer promises a surface it does not open.
- Duplicate display for a dual-record Fellow (Gloria) resolved — matched by id *and* name.
- Ballot tracker visuals sharpened in the suggestion cards, glass rows and judge cards.

**Names and the nomination ladder**
- Nominee names are now sanitised through the shared resolver: an email address can no
  longer be printed as a person's label. Fixed at the record, so every surface inherits it.
- The ladder skips any field already answered elsewhere on the record, and the scroll
  behaviour on step change was corrected — the step arrives at its head, not mid-form.
- Nominee record matching extended so a known industry figure is not re-typed from scratch.

**Also in the morning:** Page and Room navigation keys added to the Media Hub room blocks.

## 2. The hardware — the case, the speaker and the deck key

*Afternoon — 14:37 to 16:17.*

The console became something a Fellow can put down and pick back up.

**The deck key**
- A new floating key, mounted on the document rather than inside the deck, so it stands on
  the picture whatever frame, drawer or sheet the deck is drawn in. One press lowers the
  whole case — chin, wheel or radio keypad, speaker with it — and one press lifts it back.
  Nothing a surface was showing is taken away by lowering the case.
- It was placed, repositioned, then lifted out of the deck to shell level once it was clear
  the key has to outlive any one frame. Its seat clears the chin's geometry rather than
  floating over it, and it is a labelled, pressed-state control, not an icon alone.
- Recorded in `docs/handheld-deck-key.md`: what the key owns, how it behaves, what it may
  never touch (playback, routes, content), and its accessibility contract.

**The speaker frame**
- `speaker_frame` added to `FellowProfileSettings` — a device appearance choice, set from
  the Edit drawer beside the finish and the face. Display only: never read by discovery,
  ordering, measurement or scoring.
- `useSpeakerFrame` holds the preference across every mounted speaker, writes it through to
  the Fellow's own record, and reconciles local and account values on sign-in.
- The frame fitted to both speaker faces — the wheel's speaker and the radio's — so the
  moulding reads the same in either console.
- Copper focus rings on the hardware, replacing the browser's default: a key on this machine
  is pressed, and the ring says so in the house's own metal.

**The Colony's case**
- Entering `/colony` lifts the case automatically and shuts the speaker frame, and the deck
  no longer fights that state when the wheel or keypad is used inside the room.

## 3. The Colony on the desk

*Close — 16:29 to 16:31.*

The Colony was sized with the desk tube's own arithmetic: viewport minus the machine, minus
a hard-coded 41px for the menu bar. With the case lowered there is no tube to subtract from,
so the surface collapsed to its content — the world canvas got no height to draw into and the
call bar, which pins to the surface's foot, rode up under the head of the page. The black
field below it was the desk showing through.

- `DesktopShell`'s case-down branch now renders into a measured seat (`.imac-bare`) instead
  of handing the outlet to the page unwrapped.
- That seat answers the desk's existing fill contract — a surface that declares
  `data-tube-fill` fills it here exactly as it fills the tube, with no height arithmetic of
  its own.
- The Colony root now takes that contract and dropped its fixed `calc(100% - 41px)`, so it
  measures correctly with the case up or down, on the desk and on the glass.
